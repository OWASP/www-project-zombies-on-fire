- Introduce the scheduled injects, capture decisions and rationale, and use follow-up prompts where participants need more information.
- End play at the planned stopping point and reserve time for the debrief.

## Assessment rubric

Score each objective as **demonstrated**, **partially demonstrated**, or **needs practice**. Record an observed decision or artifact for each score.

- **Triage** — Who declared and owned the incident, and what evidence supported the initial scope?
- **Containment and recovery** — What did participants prioritize, what trade-offs did they identify, and how did they verify recovery?
- **Coordination** — Who needed an update or approval, when did they get it, and what was communicated?

## After-action review

- Which decisions worked well, and what made them possible?
- Where did a missing owner, procedure, or signal slow the response?
- What would the team do differently next time?
- For each follow-up, record the action, owner, due date, and evidence that will show it is complete.
